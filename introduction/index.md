I have spent the past day or so exploring the [*Instituto Geográfico Nacional*](https://www.ign.es/web/ign/portal) (*IGN*), and I have to say, that despite the plethora of available data, it is not very *FAIR* - Findable Accessible Interoperable Reusable.  The IGN labrynth has imagery and elevation tiles, OGC Features, grids, shapefiles, etc, but nothing that can tie a geospatial feature to a download link.  At least, that I can find.  Even the grids I've been able to download and display in QGIS have attributes that don't quite jive with tile names found online.
